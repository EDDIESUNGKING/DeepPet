DeepPet V8.70 – complete deployable demo

Deployment: unzip, open the dist folder and upload its contents (or the folder itself) to Netlify Drop.
The ZIP also contains the source files, JS, CSS, pet photos, five original onboarding PNGs and build.mjs.

Changes:
- Preventive care priorities open detailed pages with action and observation feedback.
- Feedback is saved in Extra Records for the selected pet.
- The Home daily summary uses mint / blue backgrounds, transparent dotted circular scores.
- Health food target, extra records and recovery have distinct styled entries.
- Me biological age compares estimated vs actual age; baseline displays a mini 14-day heart/breath trend.
- Deep relax / load scores are consistently dotted on Day, 7D and 30D.
- Deep and Health viewed-indicator state persists per pet/date; new updated alerts can reappear.
- 15 demo pet face assets are reused for fast Me avatars.

Limitations: Demo BCG metrics and risk tiers are illustrative, not veterinary diagnoses. Live SDK, Netlify and physical iPhone Safari were not verified. Existing five onboarding PNGs intentionally unchanged (English).
